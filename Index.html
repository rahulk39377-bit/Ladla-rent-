Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 21 August 2026

Ladla Rent 24x7 ("hum", "app") aapki privacy ka samman karta hai. Ye policy batati hai ki hum kaunsa data collect karte hain, kaise use karte hain, aur kaise protect karte hain.

1. Hum Kaunsa Data Collect Karte Hain

  • Booking ke liye: Naam, mobile number, pickup/drop location, booking details.
  • Partner registration ke liye: Naam, mobile number, Aadhaar number, PAN number, vehicle details — driver/rental partner verify karne ke liye.
  • Payment ke liye: UPI payment reference. Hum aapka UPI PIN, bank password ya card details kabhi collect nahi karte — payment seedha aapke UPI app se hoti hai.

2. Data Kaise Use Hota Hai

  • Booking confirm karne aur driver partner assign karne ke liye.
  • Partner ki identity verify karne ke liye (Aadhaar/PAN sirf verification ke liye, admin approval ke baad).
  • Customer support aur booking-related communication ke liye.
  • Hum aapka data kisi third-party ko bechte nahi hain.

3. Data Kahan Store Hota Hai

App ka data aapke device par (local storage) aur hamare secure systems mein store hota hai. Aadhaar/PAN sirf verification ke liye rakha jata hai aur app ke andar masked (•••• form) mein dikhaya jata hai.

4. Data Delete Karwane Ka Adhikar

Aap apna account/data delete karwa sakte hain — bas hume neeche diye contact par request bhejein. Hum 7 working days ke andar aapka data delete kar denge, jab tak legal/accounting record rakhna zaroori na ho.

5. Permissions

App koi extra device permission (camera, location tracking, contacts) forcefully access nahi karta jab tak aap khud koi feature use na karein jisme uski zaroorat ho.

6. Children's Privacy

Ye app 18 saal se kam umar ke bacchon ke liye nahi hai. Hum jaan bujh kar bacchon ka data collect nahi karte.

7. Policy Mein Badlav

Hum is policy ko samay-samay par update kar sakte hain. Koi bhi badlav is page par update kar diya jayega.
